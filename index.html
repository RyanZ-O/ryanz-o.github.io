About

I am a Ph.D. student in the Cognition X Lab at the National University of Singapore (NUS). My research focuses on semantic digital twins for on-site construction rescheduling and reconfiguration. I received my Master's degree from Dalian University of Technology (DUT) in June 2025, where I worked on construction scheduling optimization. I am broadly interested in digital construction, construction management, and AI-enabled decision support. I am always open to research collaborations. Feel free to reach out!
